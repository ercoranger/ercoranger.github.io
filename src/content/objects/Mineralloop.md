# <span class="lang-en">Introduction</span><span class="lang-zh">概念簡述</span>

<p class="col-12 lang-en project-title">Fixing minerals with minerals</p>
<p class="col-12 lang-zh project-title">用礦物質修復礦物質</p>
<p class="col-8 project-text">
  <span class="lang-en">
  Mineralloop is a collection of homeware made with discarded materials and second-hand objects from The Hague, the Netherlands. Many products that remain unsold in second-hand shops eventually end up in incinerators. In response, the project gives new life to existing glassware and ceramics by developing new coatings from locally sourced waste minerals.
  <br><br>
  But what should be designed for a world of plenty? As natural resources become increasingly depleted, Mineralloop explores alternative approaches to production by working with what already exists. Through repair and material recomposition, the project reconsiders how discarded objects and residual minerals can become resources for making, proposing a more local and circular approach to production.
  </span>

  <span class="lang-zh">
      工業製程所產生的礦物副產物，是否能重新成為修補既有物件的材料？
      Mineralloop 是一系列使用荷蘭海牙當地的剩餘材料與二手物件製作的家居用品。許多二手商店中未能售出的商品，最終仍會被送往焚化處理。因此，這項研究從既有的玻璃與陶瓷物件出發，運用海牙當地產生的礦物副產物開發新的礦物塗覆材料，讓原本可能被丟棄的物件得以重新利用。
      面對天然資源逐漸耗竭的現況，Mineralloop 嘗試從已經存在的材料與物件中尋找新的可能。透過修補與材料的重新組合，這項研究重新思考物件的製造與再造方式，探索更貼近當代處境的地方性循環製造模式。
  </span>
</p>
</div>

# <span class="lang-en">Process</span><span class="lang-zh">發展過程</span>


<p class="col-12 lang-en project-title">Circular Craft</p>
<p class="col-12 lang-zh project-title">創新工藝</p>

<p class="col-8 project-text">
  <span class="lang-en">
    Based on the research of Neo Stone Age, Erco Lai developed a new technique repairing broken glassware or ceramic with “Neostone”. From this new “kintsugi” craft, it gives those unwanted homewares a mineral coating. Erco Lai covered broken/imperfect glassware with "Neostone" developed using discarded lime pellet, powder of limestone from quarries or sand from construction sites, also known as geo-polymer, this material doesn’t require firing and adds a new touch and feeling to the object.
  </span>

  <span class="lang-zh">
    在Neo Stone Age 研究的基礎上，鈋谷設計事物所開發了一種新的工藝：用 "Neostone" 一種材料去修復破碎的玻璃器皿或陶瓷。鈋谷設計事物所使用廢棄的大理石灰顆粒、采石場的石灰石粉末或建築工地用剩的沙子開發的 "Neostone"，也被稱為地質聚合物。此新技法修復了破碎不完美的玻璃器皿，同時也賦予了物件新的觸感。另外，此技法無需燒製。
  </span>
</p>
